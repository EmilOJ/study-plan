Your goal: Apologise once, give the conclusion first, explain the cause without blaming anyone, explain why the system behaved as designed, and commit to dated countermeasures.

Model dialogue (read it aloud together first, then replace the facts with your own)

顧客先週の件、原因は分かりましたか。
あなたはい。ご迷惑をおかけして申し訳ございません。結論から申し上げますと、アンテナケーブルの前後が逆に接続されていたことが原因です。
顧客逆に？うちの整備のせいってことですか。
あなたいえ、どちらか一方の問題ではないと考えています。マストの塗装の際にケーブルを外す必要がありましたが、前後の区別が分かりにくい設計でした。
顧客それで方位が180度ずれたと。
あなたはい。前後が逆になると、二つのアンテナを結ぶ向きが反対になるので、ちょうど180度ずれます。
顧客オートパイロットが切れたのは？
あなた方位が急に変わったことをシステムが検知して、安全のために解除しました。これは設計通りの動作です。
顧客今後は大丈夫なんですか。
あなた対策は二つです。一つ目に、ケーブルに色分けのラベルを付け、逆に接続できないコネクタに変更します。二つ目に、ソフトウェアでジャイロとの比較を追加し、起動時に異常があれば警告を出します。
顧客いつまでにできますか。
あなたラベルは来週の点検時に対応します。コネクタとソフトウェアは三週間以内に対応いたします。報告書は明日お送りします。

Scenario 15 量産チームへの引き継ぎ · Handing the prototype over to QA · Week 19

品質保証量産時の検査は、どう考えていますか。

Self-worth practices and review

10 minutes of the linked daily practice, then the exercise on your lesson card.

The loop to notice: trigger → “I’m inadequate” → shame/anxiety → safety behaviour or avoidance → no chance to test the prediction. Record the trigger, thought, intensity (0–10) and response.

Main reference: The Compassionate Mind Workbook — Chris Irons & Elaine Beaumont. Read the chapter named in the lesson.

Optional: The Confidence Gap — Russ Harris.

Practices

Soothing breathing
Sit upright, slow your breathing to about 5–6 breaths a minute with a longer out-breath, and let your face and voice soften. Use the free guided audio from the Compassionate Mind Foundation (Resources) for the first weeks.
Breathing + leaves on a stream
5 min soothing breathing, then 5 min watching thoughts go by: put each thought on a leaf and let the stream carry it off, including thoughts like this is pointless. You aren't trying to make the thoughts go away, only to watch them instead of following them.
Compassionate self
2 min soothing breathing, then imagine yourself at your wisest, calmest and kindest: your posture, your face, your tone of voice, the strength to face hard things and the wish to help. Then picture the version of you who is struggling, from that position.
Compassionate self + outward attention
5 min compassionate self, then 5 min putting all your attention outside yourself: sounds near, sounds far, one at a time. This trains the shift you'll use in conversations, from monitoring how you come across to the other person and the content.
Breathing + urge surfing
5 min soothing breathing, then 5 min of open attention. When an urge comes (to check your phone, to stop), notice where it sits in your body and watch it rise and fall without acting on it.
Your choice
Whichever practice has helped most. Look at your Saturday notes to decide.

Experiment notes

  1. Prediction and belief strength (0–100%).
  2. The safety behaviour you will drop.
  3. What actually happened.
  4. Belief afterwards and how you felt an hour later.

Saturday review

Weekly review (20 min): rate (1) your peak shame or inadequacy this week, 0–10; (2) your longest stretch of replaying something; (3) how many times you said I don't know or asked a question you felt exposed asking; (4) which safety behaviours you dropped. Then: what did this week's experiments show, and what will you test next week?

When to get help

This is self-guided work, not therapy. Look for a professional instead of pushing on alone if:

  • your mood is low most days for two weeks or more, or you feel hopeless;
  • sleep, appetite or drinking change noticeably;
  • the work on origins (week 9) brings up more than you can settle after a session;
  • you have any thoughts of harming yourself. Then contact someone the same day. In Japan, the TELL Lifeline is in English: 03-5774-0992.

If you look for therapy later, ask about CFT, ACT or schema therapy, and mention the attention questions from week 10. TELL and the Japan Mental Health Professionals Network list English-speaking therapists in Japan. Your weeks of notes will make a first session much more useful.
